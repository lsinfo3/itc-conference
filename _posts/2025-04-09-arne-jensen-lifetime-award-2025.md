The International Advisory Council (IAC) of the International Teletraffic Congress (ITC) is pleased to announce that the recipient of the 2025 Arne Jensen Lifetime Award is Dr. Deep Medhi. The Arne Jensen Lifetime Award is presented at ITCs to an individual who has provided exceptional contribution to traffic modeling, control and performance, and dedication to the teletraffic community.

Recipient of the 2025 Arne Jensen Lifetime Award:<br/>
Dr. Deep Medhi
